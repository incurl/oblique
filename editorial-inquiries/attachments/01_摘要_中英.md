## 中文摘要

**题目**：基于知识库范式迁移的《楹联丛话》标注体系构建与人机协同标注流程改进

**作者**：[姓名]　　**单位**：[单位/身份]

联话（楹联话）是清代文人记录对联、评论对联的一类文体，现存者以梁章钜《楹联丛话全编》为集大成之作。此类文献既保存了大量对联文本，也保存了撰者、题署地、典故出处与品评意见等文献学信息，是研究楹联史与清代文学风尚的重要材料，但长期未被结构化处理。本文以《楹联丛话全编》为对象，在《史记》知识库（Shiji-KB）所确立的「永久编号＋类型标记＋领域校验」知识建模范式基础上，面向联话文献特性重做本体与标注规范：保留 18 类实体标记体系，**新增**联语层（以「联」为最小标注单元）、6 类关系（撰联、题于、用典、评骘、因袭、辑录）与 3 类属性（联类、品第、时代层级），并针对联话文体的对偶结构、典故指向与评骘层级提出**四条不可由叙事文献直接迁移的改造**。在流程上，本文提出「规则预标注—高不确定性优先抽检—仲裁—分层一致性审计」的人机协同流水线，配套实现 MIT 许可的度量脚本，对 Cohen's κ、Fleiss' κ、Krippendorff's α、加权 κ、Byrt 等的流行度指数与偏倚指数、Fleiss–Cohen–Everitt 渐近标准误给出统一实现，并在同一套单元定义下计算实体与关系抽取的严格/宽松 F1、配对自助法与 Wilcoxon 检验；经与 `krippendorff` 与 `scipy` 参考实现逐例对照（37 项单元测试），度量代码在名义与有序 α、Wilcoxon 精确 p 值等口径上与参考实现完全一致。在工程实现上，本文将结构化语料进一步导出为**单文件 TiddlyWiki 知识库**，可离线双击打开、支持全文检索与标签关联查询，且「导出→编译」链路可脚本化重建。语料规模为 332,547 字、2,289 个标注段落、13,135 处实体标注、2,979 个规范名、3,016 副联语；经 20 例逐例人工复核后，3,016 副（100%）上下联等长，171 副可复原集句出处冠名。**本文不报告任何未经实测的一致性数值**：κ、F1、工时等实验结果在真实人工标注完成前一律留作占位符，并以逐格映射契约建立论文数值与计算产物的对应关系。

**关键词**：联话；楹联；古籍标注；知识图谱；标注一致性；Krippendorff's α；人机协同标注

## English Abstract

**Title**: Constructing an Annotation Scheme for *Yinglian Conghua* via Knowledge-Base Paradigm Transfer, with an Improved Human-in-the-Loop Annotation Pipeline

**Author**: [Name]　　**Affiliation**: [Affiliation / Status]

Yingtong (couplet-talk) is a late-imperial Chinese genre recording couplets with their authors, sites, sources, and appraisals. Liang Zhangju's Qing collection *Yinglian Conghua Quanbian* has never been structurally processed, despite rich evidence for couplet history. This paper builds on the Shiji Knowledge Base's modelling paradigm (Purple Numbers, type-tagged entities, domain validation), but reworks it for couplet-talk. The 18-type entity inventory is inherited unchanged; the paper **adds** a couplet layer (the couplet as the atomic unit), six relation types, and three attributes (couplet class, evaluative grade, period tier), and argues that four genre properties — antithetical structure, allusive reference, graded appraisal, unstable segmentation — make that ontology non-transferable. It further specifies a human-in-the-loop pipeline (pre-annotation, uncertainty-first selection, adjudication, stratified audit) and releases an MIT-licensed script implementing Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha, weighted kappa, prevalence and bias indices, and the Fleiss–Cohen–Everitt standard error, with strict and relaxed F1 and paired tests; validation against the `krippendorff` and `scipy` packages across 37 unit tests yields exact agreement for alpha and for the exact Wilcoxon p-value. The structured corpus is further deployed as a **single-file TiddlyWiki knowledge base** that opens offline by double click, supports full-text search and tag-based cross-reference, and rebuilds byte-identically from its export-and-compile chain. The corpus comprises 332,547 characters, 2,289 annotated paragraphs, 3,016 couplets, and 13,135 entity mentions; after case-by-case review of 20 previously imbalanced entries, all 3,016 (100%) have equal upper- and lower-line lengths and 171 carry source attributions. **This paper reports no unmeasured agreement statistics**: kappa, F1, and timing results remain placeholders until human annotation is complete, while a numbers-map contract traces every figure to a computation artifact.

**Keywords**: couplet-talk; Chinese couplets; classical-text annotation; knowledge graph; inter-annotator agreement; Krippendorff's alpha; human-in-the-loop annotation
